Data safety

Your order history is never one crash away from gone

Years of bills, stock and customers live on your own computer — and the app works hard to make sure they stay there.

  • Verified backups every 30 minutes when data changes, each one checked before it's kept.
  • Closing-time backup every day, plus a reminder for the owner to download it.
  • Second copy to a USB drive or your Google Drive / OneDrive folder.
  • Backup before every delete, reset or restore — undo is always one click away.
  • Crash-safe records with automatic restart and self-repair from the newest good backup.

Questions

Frequently asked

Does it take card or UPI payments?

It records how each bill was paid — cash, UPI, card, other or a split — but it doesn't connect to a payment gateway, so there are no transaction fees. Use your existing UPI QR or card machine.

What happens if the internet goes down?

If it runs on a computer in the restaurant, nothing changes: billing, the kitchen display and the captains' phones all work over the shop Wi-Fi. Only remote access from outside pauses.

Do I need to install an app on every device?

No. Any modern browser works — Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox on a PC, tablet or phone. Open the address once and add it to the home screen if you like.

Can I try it before entering my own menu?

Yes. It starts with a full sample restaurant. When you're ready, use Settings → Data → Start fresh. The demo restaurant stays available under Admin, separate from your data.

How do I make sure I never lose data?

Backups are automatic and verified. Set a second backup folder (a USB drive or your Google Drive folder) and download the closing backup now and then — the dashboard reminds you.

Can I run more than one restaurant?

Yes. One installation can run several restaurants, each with its own menu, staff, data and backups. Staff pick their restaurant on the sign-in screen.

Does it print bills and KOTs?

Yes, through the browser's print dialog, so any printer your computer can use — including 58 mm and 80 mm thermal printers — works.
